# Interview Script — PJ Hybrid (45–60 min)

> Rules: record with permission · ask about PAST BEHAVIOR, not opinion · silence is a tool · only present the Tally concept in the last 10 min · one question at a time.

## Opening (5 min)

- Introduction: "I'm researching how self-employed people deal with money; there's no right answer".
- Tell me what you do and how your work works (CLT before? how long as a PJ? how do you bill?).

## Block 1 — The real financial life (15 min)

- Walk me through your last client payment: where did it land? What did you do with the money in the first 24h?
- How do you know today how much you can spend on your personal life in a month?
- What happens when the DAS/tax time comes? Tell me about the last time.
- Do you separate PF and PJ accounts? How, in practice? (a separate bank? the same account? "in your head"?)
- Have you ever been in a jam from mixing company and personal money? Tell me that story.
- In a month when you bill well, what changes in your behavior? And in a bad month?

## Block 2 — Tools and attempts (15 min)

- What do you use TODAY to track money? (ask them to open and show it, if remote: share the screen)
- Have you ever tried a finance app? Which one? What made you **stop** using it? ⭐ (the golden question — dig deeper)
- And a spreadsheet? Who built it? Is it alive or abandoned?
- Do you pay for any tool for your work today? Which and how much? (an indirect anchor of willingness to pay)
- Your accountant: what do they do for you? What would you like them to do?

## Block 3 — Family (if applicable, 5 min)

- Do you split household bills with someone? How does the settling work?
- Has there ever been friction/an argument over money visibility? Tell me.

## Block 4 — Reactions to the concept (10 min — only now!)

Show 1–2 prototype screens OR verbally describe the income splitter:

> "Imagine: R$ 5,000 came in from a client. The app tells you right away: R$ 300 is tax, R$ 750 goes to reserve, R$ 3,950 is your available salary."

- What went through your head? (silence)
- What would you do with that information?
- What would need to be true for you to trust these numbers?
- If this cost the price of a snack per month, what would you think? (observe the reaction, not the polite answer)

## Closing (5 min)

- If you had a magic wand to solve ONE thing about your money, what would it be?
- Do you know anyone else who lives off a CNPJ and would be up for a chat? (snowball)
- Can I reach out when the beta is ready?

## Anti-questions (NEVER ask)

- ❌ "Would you use an app that...?" (everyone says yes)
- ❌ "Do you think it's a good idea?" (polite praise ≠ data)
- ❌ "How much would you pay?" right at the start (without context, the answer is fantasy)
